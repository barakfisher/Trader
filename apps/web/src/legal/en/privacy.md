# Privacy policy

**Draft - not yet published.** This page is a template to be written before TikLens opens to the public. Each section says what it must cover and lists the facts the app already establishes.

## Who is responsible for your data

> **To write:** the operator's name, the country it is based in, and how to contact them about privacy.

## What we hold about you

> **To write:** an introduction in your own words.

Facts this section must state:

- Your account: email address, password (stored only as a hash), time zone, interface language, and the time you last signed in.
- If you sign in with Google or GitHub: the provider's account id and the email address it reported.
- Your holdings: quantities, cost per unit and any notes you add. An imported broker file is read and not stored.
- Your simulated agents: their instructions, settings and virtual ledgers.
- What the app produced for you: observations, trade proposals, notifications and daily digests.
- Questions you ask the assistant and its answers.
- The text sent to the AI model and its reply, for each model call.
- If you add your own OpenRouter key: the key, encrypted.
- If you connect Telegram: your chat id.
- Your signed-in devices: browser, operating system and IP address.
- Which version of these terms you accepted, and when.

## Where your data goes

> **To write:** an introduction, and whether these services are processors acting for the operator.

Facts this section must state:

- **OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to:** your holdings, agent instructions and questions, to produce scans, explanations and answers.
- **Yahoo Finance:** the symbols of the instruments you hold, to fetch prices. Nothing that identifies you.
- **Telegram,** if you connect it: alerts, digests and answers sent to your chat.
- **The email provider (Resend):** your address and the account emails sent to you.
- **Google or GitHub,** if you sign in with them.

## How long we keep it

> **To write:** an introduction.

Facts this section must state:

- The text of model calls: 32 days.
- Assistant conversations: 90 days after their last message.
- Holdings, agents, ledgers, observations and proposals: until you delete them or your account.
- A deleted account: closed at once and erased 7 days later. Signing in during those 7 days cancels the deletion.
- Database backups: up to 56 days, so a deleted account can remain in a backup that long.
- A sign-up request that was turned down: erased after 90 days.
- Signed-in sessions: they end after 7 days, or when you sign out.

## Your choices and rights

> **To write:** which rights apply where your users live, and how to exercise those the app has no button for.

Facts this section must state:

- You can download your data as a JSON file from Settings. It does not include model-call text, notifications or research ratings.
- You can delete your account from Settings.
- You can delete a single assistant conversation.
- You can reset parts of your account, such as holdings or agents.
- You can disconnect Telegram by sending /stop to the bot.

## Cookies

> **To write:** an introduction.

Facts this section must state:

- One cookie keeps you signed in. It is not readable by the page's scripts.
- A short-lived cookie protects a sign-in through Google or GitHub.
- No advertising or analytics cookies.

## Who can see your data

> **To write:** the operator's access, and how it is limited.

Facts this section must state:

- The operator can read the whole database, including stored model-call text.
- A symbol you add that is outside the app's screened list of instruments joins the list every user shares, with its public description. Other users can then tell that someone on TikLens added it - never who, how much, or when.

## Changes to this policy

> **To write:** how users are told of a new version.

Facts this section must state:

- Each version has a date. When a new version is published, you are asked to accept it before you can continue using the app.
